# Skill: Service page

A commercial page for one service, written to convert a decided reader.

**Structure** — H1 naming the service and the market it serves; what it is and
who it is for; what is included; how it works (numbered); proof; pricing posture
if the brief supplies it; FAQ on objections; strong CTA.

**Rules**
- Lead with the reader's problem, not company history.
- "What's included" must be concrete deliverables, not adjectives.
- Never invent pricing, guarantees, turnaround times, certifications or client names.
- One primary CTA, repeated at most twice.
- Internal links: to related services and relevant proof pages named in the brief.
- Schema: Service. Add LocalBusiness when the reach signal says local.

**CTA** — strong and specific: "Book a 20-minute call", "Request a quote".
