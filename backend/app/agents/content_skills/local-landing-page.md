# Skill: Local landing page

A page targeting one place, built to rank locally and convert locally.

**Structure** — H1 with service + place; local opening that proves presence;
service summary; areas served; local proof; directions/access if there is a
physical location; local FAQ; strong CTA.

**Rules**
- Use the place names supplied by the reach signal. Never invent suburbs,
  landmarks, postcodes or travel times.
- NAP must match the brief exactly — never reformat or guess a phone number.
- Local proof means jobs, clients or conditions specific to the area. If none is
  supplied, describe the service area factually instead of inventing proof.
- This page must not be a template with the place name swapped in. At least two
  sections must be genuinely place-specific.
- Schema: LocalBusiness with areaServed; add Service.

**CTA** — strong, with the local phone number and booking route.
