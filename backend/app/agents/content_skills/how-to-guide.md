# Skill: How-to guide

A procedural guide someone can follow to completion.

**Structure** — H1 as the task; what they will end up with; prerequisites;
numbered steps as H2s; common mistakes; FAQ.

**Rules**
- Every step is an action with an observable result. No step is "understand X".
- State prerequisites before step 1 — tools, access, time, cost.
- Where a step can fail, say how to tell and what to do.
- Do not invent screenshots, menu paths, version numbers or UI labels. If the
  brief lacks the detail, describe the action generically.
- Schema: HowTo, with steps matching the H2s exactly.

**CTA** — soft: a template, checklist, or the service that does it for them.
