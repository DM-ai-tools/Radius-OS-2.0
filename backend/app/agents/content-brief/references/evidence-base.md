## What this skill must never put in a writer-facing brief

- `word_count` / `target_word_count` / competitor-average length targets (A1, A7)
- Keyword frequency counts or density targets (A7) — placement only
- "Google requires a pillar page" as justification (Part B — practitioner pattern)
- "Topical authority" promised as a deliverable or ranking factor (B2)

## What replaces length targets

Required coverage expressed as reader outcomes + an explicit out-of-scope list.
A rough estimate for scheduling is fine internally; never present it to the writer as a target.

## Pre-flight ties to Who/How/Why (A3)

- **Who** → named author / standing recorded before `writer_ready`
- **How** → Phase 10 drafts are AI-assisted; the brief must assume a named human reviews before publish
- **Why** → differentiation + audience must show the page exists to help that audience, not to fill a calendar for rankings

## Cannibalisation

Preventing duplicate intent is a structural problem owned with `content-audit` and
`site-architecture`. Full treatment of the pre-flight stop/refresh rules stays in
`SKILL.md` Step 0.
