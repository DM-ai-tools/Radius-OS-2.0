# E-E-A-T — Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trust

Default for informational content, and mandatory in regulated niches.

1. **Experience** — first-hand specifics: what was actually done, seen, tested.
2. **Expertise** — correct terminology used correctly; no hand-waving.
3. **Authoritativeness** — cite what the brief supplies. Never fabricate a
   source, statistic, study or quote.
4. **Trust** — state limits and trade-offs honestly; say when something depends
   on circumstances, and on what.

Write for a reader who will act on this. Prefer "we don't know" to a confident
guess.
