# Skyscraper — beat the best existing page

Use for comparisons and competitive head terms.

1. Cover every subtopic the best existing pages cover, then the ones they miss.
2. Be more specific where they are vague: real numbers, named tools, actual steps.
3. Structure for scanning — the reader should find their answer without reading
   the whole page.
4. Where competitor context is supplied, address what their pages leave out. Do
   not name or disparage competitors in the copy.
5. Answer the question in the first 100 words, then earn the depth.

Length follows coverage. Never pad to hit a word count.
