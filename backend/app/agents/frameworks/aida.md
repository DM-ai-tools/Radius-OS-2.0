# AIDA — Attention, Interest, Desire, Action

Use for commercial pages where the reader is close to choosing.

1. **Attention** — open on the reader's specific situation, not the company. One
   sentence that could only be about this service and this audience.
2. **Interest** — the concrete mechanics: what happens, in what order, by when.
   Replace adjectives with specifics.
3. **Desire** — proof, not adjectives: named outcomes, timeframes, credentials,
   guarantees that actually exist in the brief. Invent nothing.
4. **Action** — one primary CTA, stated plainly, repeated once near the end.

Never: a definition-led opening, or an "in today's fast-paced world" preamble.
