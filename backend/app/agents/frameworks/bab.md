# BAB — Before, After, Bridge

Use for consideration-stage content where the reader needs to see the change.

1. **Before** — the reader's current state, described so they recognise it.
2. **After** — the state once it is solved. Specific and believable; no
   guaranteed outcomes or invented numbers.
3. **Bridge** — how they get from one to the other, and the role of this service
   or method in that crossing.

Keep the bridge practical enough to act on even without buying.
