# StoryBrand — Hero, Problem, Guide, Plan, Action, Stakes

The reader is the hero; the business is the guide, never the hero.

1. **Hero** — the reader and what they want.
2. **Problem** — external (practical), internal (how it feels), and why it is
   unfair that it is this hard.
3. **Guide** — the business shows empathy and competence, briefly.
4. **Plan** — three to four named steps so the path feels finishable.
5. **Action** — one direct CTA.
6. **Stakes** — what is won by acting and lost by not. Stay proportionate.
