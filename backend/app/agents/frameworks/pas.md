# PAS — Problem, Agitate, Solve

Use for decision-stage service pages where the reader already feels the pain.

1. **Problem** — name the problem in the reader's own words in the first two
   sentences. No preamble, no definitions.
2. **Agitate** — the cost of leaving it alone: what it keeps costing, what it
   blocks, what it risks. Concrete and specific; never fear-mongering, and never
   a manufactured statistic.
3. **Solve** — the service as the resolution. What it does, what the reader gets,
   what the first step is.

Close with a strong, single CTA. Keep agitation shorter than the solution.
